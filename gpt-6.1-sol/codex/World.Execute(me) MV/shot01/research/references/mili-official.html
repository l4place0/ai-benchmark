Limited Premium Package Edition

 

SIHT1004　
3,000 Yen (tax included)

2 years have passed since Mili's previous album Mag Mell.

Anticipated by fans around the globe, Mili's 2nd album Miracle Milk will be featuring songs from the popular rhythm game Deemo, such as "YIBIKIRI-GENMAN" and "Space Colony", to their new Youtube hit songs like "world.execute(me);" and "Ga1ahad and Scientific Witchery".
Besides the hits, there will also be unreleased tracks that aren't available elsewhere, making the number of tracks a total of 18.

The Limited Premium Package Edition includes 18 artistically drawn original illustrated lyrics cards that portrays the magical world of Mili.
